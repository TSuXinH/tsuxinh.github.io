About

Computational neuroscience, NeuroAI, and reusable representations of neural population dynamics.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Institute for Brain and Cognitive Sciences and the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Qionghai Dai.

My research lies at the intersection of computational neuroscience and machine learning. I am particularly interested in how models trained on large-scale calcium-imaging recordings can generalize across sessions, animals, datasets, experimental paradigms, and species—and how the resulting representations can reveal functional organization in neural systems.

Research themes

  • Neural population dynamics and forecasting
  • Foundation-style models for functional neural recordings
  • Cross-dataset and cross-species representation learning
  • NeuroAI and biologically grounded machine learning
  • Computational analysis of behavior and neural function

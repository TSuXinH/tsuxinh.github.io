👋 Hi, I’m Xinhong.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Institute for Brain and Cognitive Sciences and the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Qionghai Dai.

I work at the intersection of computational neuroscience and machine learning, with a focus on reusable models of large-scale neural population dynamics.
